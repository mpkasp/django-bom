Connect an AI assistant

An assistant such as Claude can search your parts, read BOMs and edit them through IndaBOM's MCP server. It acts as you, in your organization, and never has more access than your role or the level you grant it.

{{ mcp_url }}

Sign in from your assistant

Add the server, then sign in. Your browser opens, you sign in to IndaBOM as usual, and a page asks what the assistant may do. No token to copy.

Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http indabom {{ mcp_url }}

Then run /mcp inside Claude Code, choose indabom and select Authenticate.

claude.ai and Claude Desktop

Open Settings → Connectors, choose Add custom connector, enter the address above, then connect and sign in.

Cursor

Add this to ~/.cursor/mcp.json, then choose Connect next to indabom in Cursor's MCP settings.

{"mcpServers": {"indabom": {"url": "{{ mcp_url }}"}}}

What the assistant may do

  • Read: look up parts, BOMs and where parts are used.
  • Edit: also edit Draft revisions, revise Released parts, and build and submit change orders. Edits are saved at once and recorded in the part's history.
  • Edit and release: also release revisions and approve or reject change orders, as you. The assistant cannot approve a change order you wrote or edited unless you are the only member.

To change a Released part, the assistant revises it, edits the new Draft, and puts the Draft in a change order for review. A person approves it in IndaBOM, or, at the Edit and release level, the assistant approves it for you after asking. Releasing and approving are not possible at the Read or Edit level.

{% if oauth_enabled %}You choose the level when you sign in. A token's level is set when you create it.{% else %}A token's level is set when you create it.{% endif %} The assistant never has more access than your role: Viewers can only choose Read.

Disconnecting

Revoke the connection or token in Settings → Connected apps. The assistant stops working at once.{% if oauth_enabled %} Connected apps are labeled as such; to reconnect, sign in from the assistant again.{% endif %}
